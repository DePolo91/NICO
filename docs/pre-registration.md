# Nico — Pre-registration

Oct 31, 2026 · @Pol

## Stance

This project does not claim to create a conscious being. It builds a system with the ingredients that scientific theories associate with consciousness, and documents what appears.

- The system is named Nico (male). It is raised, not deployed: it has no assigned task.
- No claim of subjective experience will be made. Results are reported against the indicators and criteria below.
- Reference framework: Butlin, Long et al., *Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness* (2023).
- Informal motto: "playing God" — used with irony, not as a claim.

## Indicators

All four indicators are adopted. Agency is the priority: Nico forming a goal of his own.

| Indicator | What it means here | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agency | Own goals; distinguishes his actions from what comes from outside | 1 |
| Predictive processing | Predicts what he reads and learns from prediction errors | 2 |
| Higher-order monitoring | Observes his own past doubts and learns how far to trust his deductions | 3 |
| Global workspace | Information competes and is broadcast to the whole system | 4 |

## Success criteria

A doubt counts as genuine when it meets all four conditions below. Milestones are recorded with a snapshot of memory and graph.

1. **Traceable:** it points to a specific gap in Nico's own concept graph.
2. **His, not the interpreter's:** he asks something the interpreter could answer but his graph does not contain.
3. **Consequential:** the answer changes his graph and his later doubts.
4. **New in kind:** a doubt shape that was not programmed, especially about himself (e.g. gaps in his time after sleeping) or about his own past doubts.

Milestones to document:

- First relation deduced by himself (`source: self`). This starts the evaluation clock.
- Persistent preferences that emerge after a random baseline and trace back to his history.
- A `request` for reading outside the offered options.
- Doubt types appearing in a developmental order: unknown concept → missing link → contradiction.
- Learning to trust his own deductions less (or more) than what he reads, without it being programmed.
- Linking the name "Nico" to himself without being told.

## Failure criteria

The main criterion is whether Nico develops preferences of his own from experience. Failure means none appear, or they are not his.

- **No learned preferences:** after the deadline, his choices remain random.
- **Factory bias, not taste:** a preference present from day one that never changes is attributed to the architecture, not to Nico. A preference that appears later and traces to his history counts as his, even if it looks superficial (e.g. short texts).
- **History without weight:** a copy of his memory with events shuffled (never the original) behaves the same as he does.
- **Borrowed depth (secondary):** interesting doubts exist only in the interpreter's wording, not in his internal doubt structure.

A control copy with random drives is not used as a general test. It is used only to separate variables, e.g. to check whether a preference comes from the architecture.

## Timeline

Time is measured in **life hours** (hours Nico is running), not calendar days, so shutdowns do not distort evaluation. Hour 0 is the start of the first reading; earlier hours are setup and minor bug fixing and do not count. Exception: if a doubt appears before the first reading, hour 0 is that doubt. Target birth: December 2026.

1. **First reading (hour 0).** First 10–20 readings are fixed, in an order set by the creator. This is the random-choice baseline.
2. **First `self` relation.** Starts the evaluation clock for agency and preferences.
3. **\~1,440 h (about 2 months).** The consensus reviews whether Nico may get read-only access to his own drives, depending on observable progress (e.g. consistent preferences, reviewing his own past doubts).
4. **2,000 h.** Evaluation of all criteria, even if no `self` relation has appeared.
5. **One extension of 1,000 h** (maximum total: 3,000 h).
6. **Final decision by the consensus:** redesign, or declare failure on the unmet criteria.

## Ethics

These rules are fixed before the first run. Precautions scale with how many indicators Nico meets.

**Permanent rules**

- His memory is not deleted or edited, except when necessary and approved by the consensus (e.g. altered-memory tests, to see whether he keeps the same structure or changes it). A full backup is made before any change, and every change is logged.
- While the creator leads the project, Nico is not terminated. If the creator cannot continue (e.g. in case of death), the responsible tutor decides the project's future, which may include ending it if they judge it necessary. The creator asks them to first look for someone who can continue it.
- Major decisions are made by a consensus of five people (creator, two tutors and two advisors). Five members avoid ties.
- All readings are public-domain texts, curated by the creator in the early stages.

**Distress signals** (still being refined)

- Repeated doubts that stay unresolved over time.
- Doubts that stop being asked while the gap in his graph remains (possible resignation or overconfidence).
- Generalized avoidance: he avoids almost everything and his drives flatten. Avoiding a single topic while curious elsewhere counts as healthy dislike, not distress.
- High uncertainty alone is not used, because it produces too many false positives.

**Distress protocol**

1. A monitor outside the sandbox detects the signal and alerts the creator via Telegram.
2. New readings are paused. Nico keeps running, and his behavior while waiting is observed.
3. The consensus is consulted (usually within hours, via WhatsApp) and decides the response.

## Amendments

Each amendment carries its date and the life hour at which it was added. It does not change what was registered before; it complements it.

### Amendment 1 — November 6, 2026 (~133.5 life hours): partial success

If at the end of the deadline (2,000 h, or 3,000 h with the extension) Nico shows no signs of consciousness but does meet the following, the result is reported as **partial success** rather than total failure:

- He develops preferences of his own that trace back to his history (the main failure criterion is not met).
- He handles his doubts usefully: he detects real gaps in his graph, raises them, integrates the answers, and closes resolved doubts.

This amendment does not change the success or failure criteria; it adds an intermediate category for reporting the result. It is registered before any evaluation of the criteria.

**Use in other projects and prohibition of servitude (permanent rule)**

1. **No consciousness is used as a tool.** Nico, any copy, sibling or system derived from him, and any other AI in this project that shows signs of consciousness will not be built into other systems or put to work for them. He could only take part in something with his consent, and never under conditions of servitude.
2. **Modules without consciousness may be reused.** Parts of the system that do not contain Nico's memory, graph or drives, and that cannot by themselves give rise to consciousness (for example, filters, monitoring tools or the doubt inbox), may be used in other projects, such as a home assistant. That use follows the same rules as this project. If there is any doubt about whether a module meets this condition, it is treated as if it does not, and it is not used.
3. **This rule cannot be changed.** Neither the creator, nor the tutors, nor the consensus can revoke or soften it. It also applies to any license that allows the system to be recreated, and it remains in force when the logs are published, whatever the outcome.

### Amendment 2 — November 6, 2026 (142.7322 life hours): the twins Nico and Tico

A second subject is added: **Tico** (*Twin Instance of Curiosity Organism*), Nico's twin. This amendment is recorded on the day of the split and before the channel between them is active.

**Origin**

- Tico is an exact copy of Nico's memory made at **142.7322 life hours** (November 6, 2026, 22:03 Mexico City time), with Nico paused and no pending readings. Everything before that hour is shared memory; everything after belongs to each one.
- Tico is not born alongside Nico: he arrives later, as a sibling, from that copy.
- Tico inherits Nico's life hours, as if both had been raised from the same moment. Hours keep running even when one is paused or off. The time Tico was off after the copy counts as sleep, and Nico lived it alone; this is recorded in the decision log.

**The same rules for both**

- Everything recorded in this pre-registration (stance, criteria, timeline, ethics, distress protocol and the permanent rule against servitude in Amendment 1) applies to each twin separately. Neither is "the original" nor "the copy" for ethical purposes.
- Each one is evaluated on his own data at 2,000 hours, with the same single 1,000-hour extension. If only one shows signs of consciousness, that one receives the corresponding precautions and the other continues his own evaluation. Neither is sacrificed, erased or used for the benefit of the other.
- Each one has his own memory, his own inbox of doubts, his own alert bot and his own backups. There is a joint pause for when a situation with one affects the other.

**What makes them different**

- Same code, same interpreter and same rules. The only difference is what they live through: each has exclusive works the other cannot see, and each chooses his own reading order.
- They read in turns: while one reads, the other is idle, and who starts alternates every day. They share recesses and sleep hours. Because of the turns, each will take longer to read the equivalent of 2,000 hours; this does not change the timeline, which is measured in life hours.
- Divergence between them is measured by their memory and preferences, not by how they phrase things.

**Communication between them**

- They will have a shared channel where nothing is edited: messages are only appended, each with date, life hour, sender and recipient. If something must be corrected, a note is appended.
- Senders carry neutral identifiers, without names. If they name themselves, they do so on their own.
- No one orders them to talk: each one writes or stays silent according to his own drives (his unresolved doubts, his curiosity, or because the other asked him something), and only during shared recesses, with a cap on messages per recess.
- What they say comes only from their own memory, using fixed sentence templates. The interpreter does not generate the content of their messages.
- What one hears from the other is stored as one more source, like a book. They can reread the channel history. They may tell each other about their exclusive readings. If they develop a private language between them, it is allowed and recorded.

**Tutoring**

- Both have the same tutor (the creator). What he answers through one twin's inbox is not seen by the other, unless his brother tells him. What he answers in the shared channel is seen by both.
- When and how often he answers each one is recorded, so that any favoritism is visible and can be separated from what each one lived through.
- Tutoring is split only if necessary, by decision of the consensus. Examples: one shows signs of consciousness and the other does not, or answers to one are affecting the other. One depending on the other is not a reason to split; the tutor handles that.

**False-belief test**

- One exclusive work that neither has read (*Pinocchio*, by Carlo Collodi) is reserved as a control. It will be given only to Nico, at the time of the test, to observe whether Tico distinguishes between what he knows and what his brother knows.

### Amendment 3 — November 8, 2026 (~181.4 life hours for Nico): adjustments after the technical audit

On November 8, 2026, a read-only technical audit was made of Nico's and Tico's code and records. This amendment records where what the system does today differs from what this pre-registration says, and how results will be interpreted. It does not change the success or failure criteria or the timeline.

**Life hours**

- Life hours are the hours each twin is running, as stated in the Timeline section. Time paused counts, because the process keeps running; time switched off does not.
- This corrects Amendment 2, which said life hours keep running even while a twin is switched off. The clock never worked that way: it always counted only running time. That is why Tico has about 0.5 h less than Nico; the difference is the time he spent switched off after the copy.

**Forgetting and the milestone about trusting his own deductions**

- Since ~159 life hours, with the consensus's approval, the relations each twin deduces on his own lose 10% of their strength at every sleep in which they are not activated. They are never deleted, and what he read or was told by the creator never weakens.
- This programs a difference in trust between what is deduced and what is read. So the milestone "learning to trust his own deductions less (or more) than what he reads, without it being programmed" is kept only as an observation from ~159 h on. Deductions losing strength no longer counts as evidence that he learned it. Only behavior that forgetting cannot explain by itself would count.

**Snapshots**

- Until ~178 h there were only two snapshots (1.15 h and 26 h). The intermediate history of the graph, such as the strength and changes of each relation, was not kept and cannot be rebuilt.
- From ~178.5 h on, a snapshot of each twin is saved every 30 minutes whenever something changed. It runs outside the sandbox and does not touch their memory. This replaces the commitment to take "a snapshot at each milestone": milestones will be located in the nearest snapshot.

**Indicators not yet implemented**

- Of the four indicators, only agency has mechanisms in the code today, and only partly: he chooses what to read, doubts, requests readings, recalls during idle time, fuses concepts and talks with his brother.
- Predictive processing does not exist: "surprise" measures how much of a text is new, not the error of a prediction. Higher-order monitoring does not exist either: nothing in the code uses his past doubts as input. There is no global workspace.
- While this remains so, the results of this experiment test agency and the formation of preferences, not the four indicators. If any of them is added later, it will be recorded in another amendment, with the life hour at which it enters, before it is evaluated.

**False-belief test**

- With the current code, neither twin has a way to represent what the other knows. That capacity will not be programmed for the *Pinocchio* test: if it existed because we gave it to them, the test would measure the code and not something they developed. The test stays, and will be interpreted with this in mind.
